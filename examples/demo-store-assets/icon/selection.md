# Icon candidate selection

## Batch 1 — rejected as generic

The first six-candidate batch was preserved, but it was not promoted because
the strongest result depended on a checkmark inside a rounded window. That
communicates category and completion quickly, but it is not distinctive enough
to serve as an ownable product mark.

## Batch 2 — selected direction

Six independent candidates were generated from the Example App context with
literal category symbols excluded:

| Candidate | Direction | Read at 32×32 | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Rounded signal companion | Yes | Keep as alternate |
| A2 | Open visibility slabs | Yes | Keep as alternate |
| B1 | Open task doorway | Yes | Keep as alternate |
| B2 | Rounded window companion | Yes | Keep as alternate |
| C1 | Aperture arch with crescent | Yes | Keep as alternate |
| C2 | Asymmetric visibility lens | Yes | Selected |

## Selection rationale

Select **C2** because the asymmetric cobalt aperture, coral crescent, and small
offset tail create a repeatable silhouette for “keep important tasks visible”
without borrowing a generic checklist, document, bell, or lock symbol. It
remains legible at 180px and 32px. The generated C2 was then redrawn as
`icon-master.svg` so the final asset has exact colors, controlled curves, and
reliable raster exports.

## Review notes

- The generated candidates remain available under `concepts-v2/` for audit and
  future iteration.
- The vector refinement is a demo-quality direction, not a trademark claim.
- The first batch remains under `concepts/` to show why the distinctiveness
  gate exists.

## Review status

This is a demo selection, not production approval. Confirm trademark
distinctiveness, platform treatment, and product-owner approval before using it
outside this fixture.
