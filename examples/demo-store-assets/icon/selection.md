# Icon candidate selection

## Batch 1 — rejected as generic

The first six-candidate batch was preserved, but it was not promoted because
the strongest result depended on a checkmark inside a rounded window. That
communicates category and completion quickly, but it is not distinctive enough
to serve as an ownable product mark.

## Batch 2 — rejected as too flat

The second batch improved distinctiveness but still depended on flat geometric
forms. It was retained as a useful exploration, not promoted as the store
icon.

## Batch 3 — selected direction

Six independent candidates were generated from the Example App context with
literal category symbols excluded and a premium 3D art direction:

| Candidate | Direction | Read at 32×32 | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Asymmetric cobalt portal with coral core | Yes | Selected |
| A2 | Coral visor companion | Yes | Keep as alternate |
| B1 | Folded cobalt cradle | Yes | Keep as alternate |
| B2 | Cobalt doorway sculpture | Mostly | Keep as alternate |
| C1 | Coral focus stone with cobalt ribbon | Yes | Keep as alternate |
| C2 | Cobalt shelter with coral core | Yes | Keep as alternate |

## Selection rationale

Select **A1** because the asymmetric cobalt portal and coral core form a
recognizable object at 32px while retaining enough material depth to feel like
a finished product surface at store size. The open cradle communicates “keep
something important in view” without borrowing a generic checklist, document,
bell, or lock symbol. A1 was refined once for centering, consistent bevels,
contained lighting, and a quieter background before raster exports.

## Review notes

- The generated candidates remain available under `concepts-v3/` for audit and
  future iteration.
- The 3D refinement is a demo-quality direction, not a trademark claim.
- The first two batches remain under `concepts/` and `concepts-v2/` to show why the distinctiveness
  gate exists.

## Review status

This is a demo selection, not production approval. Confirm trademark
distinctiveness, platform treatment, and product-owner approval before using it
outside this fixture.
