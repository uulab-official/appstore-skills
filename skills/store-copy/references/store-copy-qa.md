# Store copy QA checklist

## Evidence and product fit

- Map each feature, benefit, and named audience to a project source.
- Remove claims supported only by comments, mockups, or unused code.
- Compare the copy with the actual icon, screenshots, onboarding, and product
  behavior.
- Confirm pricing, trial, account, permission, and access language when in
  scope.

## Platform checks

- Verify current official Apple and Google field limits and policy guidance.
- Check required fields, character counts, punctuation, and unsupported
  markup.
- Keep Apple and Google drafts separate when their fields or audience differ.
- Check the release notes against the actual change set.

## Editorial checks

- Use one clear value proposition per field.
- Remove filler, repeated keywords, empty superlatives, and ambiguous jargon.
- Check spelling, capitalization, numbers, units, and product names.
- Keep claims and terminology stable for the localization workflow.

## Delivery checks

- Preserve the prior approved copy before replacing it.
- Record locale, source evidence, platform documentation checked, and approval
  status.
- Mark unresolved policy or product questions as `blocked` or `review`, not
  `verified`.
