# Marketing template QA

Use this checklist after filling a promotional or feature-graphic template and
before exporting channel variants.

## Brief fidelity

- Confirm the headline and supporting line come from the app project, approved
  copy, or an explicit user brief.
- Map the hero asset and every supporting cue to `brand-context.yml` or a
  supplied asset path.
- Record assumptions and unresolved approvals in the template and package QA.

## Composition

- Keep all copy and important mark details inside the declared `safe_area`.
- Review the full artboard, a 25% thumbnail, and a narrow crop.
- Preserve one clear focal point; remove decorative details that compete with
  the product mark or claim.
- Keep materials, lighting, and depth consistent with the approved icon and
  screenshot system.

## Claims and output

- Reject fabricated ratings, reviews, awards, user counts, rankings, and
  guarantees.
- Keep promotional, feature-graphic, and platform-specific masters separate.
- Declare `format` and `dimensions` for every rendered image in `manifest.yml`.
- Verify current platform rules at execution time and record the documentation
  URL and date; a working artboard is not proof of submission readiness.
- Mark the template `review` until product approval and platform checks exist.
